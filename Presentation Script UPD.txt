Slide 6 — Objective 1 Model Development (5:45–8:00)
Aayush

“For Objective 1, our goal was interpretability and inference.”

Josh

“We initially fit a full multiple linear regression model using all available predictors.”

Aayush

“We evaluated statistical significance, VIF values, AIC, BIC, and diagnostic plots.”

Josh

“One major issue we discovered was severe multicollinearity involving Beds, AvgDailyCensus, and AvgNurses.”

Aayush

“In particular, Beds and AvgDailyCensus had a correlation of approximately 0.98. Since Beds was also statistically insignificant, we removed it from the model.”

Josh

“We then removed additional predictors with weak statistical evidence to improve model parsimony while maintaining predictive performance.”

Slide 7 — Final Objective 1 Model (8:00–10:00)
Aayush

“This slide shows our final Objective 1 model.”

Josh

“The final model achieved an R² of approximately 0.614 and an adjusted R² of 0.585, with an overall model p-value below 0.001.”

Aayush

“The most important finding involved Infection Risk.”

Josh

“Holding all other variables constant, a one-unit increase in infection risk was associated with an estimated increase of approximately 0.456 days in average hospital stay.”

Aayush

“We also observed significant regional effects, along with associations involving age, average daily census, and nursing staff.”

Slide 8 — Diagnostics & Conclusion (10:00–11:30)

Aayush
